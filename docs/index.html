CIIM-SAI

A simulation of the costs and operational requirements of SAI deployment.

Inputs: A pattern and schedule of SAI deployment, and assumptions about the deployment infrastructure being used.
Outputs: Costs, operational requirements, and procurement schedule.

Two deployment methods are implemented. Balloons are the real one: disposable latex weather balloons filled with hydrogen and a payload gas, launched from facilities of launchpads and bursting at the injection altitude. Treat those figures as preliminary — the largest single cost is the balloons themselves, at an assumed unit price well below what small quantities retail for today. The "teleporter" is fictional, kept because a deliberately simple method is useful for exercising the model. Source code can be found at github.com/samkaufmann1/CIIM.
