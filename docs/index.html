CIIM-SAI

A simulation of the costs and operational requirements of SAI deployment.

Inputs: A pattern and schedule of SAI deployment, and assumptions about the deployment infrastructure being used.
Outputs: Costs, operational requirements, and procurement schedule.

Currently only a fictional "teleporter" deployment method is implemented. Source code can be found at github.com/samkaufmann1/CIIM.
